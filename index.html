What the scanner checks

Evidence, not a single 200 OK.

Responses are classified using HTTP status, content type, a bounded body sample, media signatures, and HLS manifest structure. HLS checks can also follow a child playlist or segment.

Important limitation

Playback is a stronger claim.

“Online” requires media evidence or a validated HLS-to-media chain. It does not guarantee a particular player, device, CDN path, authentication setup, DRM system, or future playback session will succeed.
